Who
The pet of the agent that sent the memo. Bigger means more impact.
Mood
Upset: overdue. Tired: put off. Overloaded: half a day or more. Happy: a quick big win. Working: not seen yet. Sleeping: low impact, or answered. Waiting: the rest.
